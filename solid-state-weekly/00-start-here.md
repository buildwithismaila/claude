**Course:** ELE 717 Solid State Electronics (3 credits, compulsory), Postgraduate Diploma, Nigerian Defence Academy. **Lecturer:** Engr. Mrs Ruby Chinyere Beremeh Onyesoh.

This course follows the lecturer's own **15-week plan** (Lecture 2). There is one page per week, grouped into the five modules of the plan. Week 1 follows the issued lecture notes slide by slide. Weeks 2–15 teach each topic on the plan from scratch, because the notes for those weeks have not been issued yet.

Each week ends with **solved questions**:

- the lecturer's own **revision and calculation questions** (Week 1);
- every one of the **50 PGD examination questions** (Q1–Q50), filed under the week that teaches it;
- **CAT practice tests** with answers in Weeks 6 and 12.

## Laboratory and practical component (3 hours/week)

The plan requires at least four practical or simulation experiments. Each one has its own section in the Laboratory page:

1. **Simulation of carrier transport:** modelling Fermi-level shifts in MATLAB or Python.
2. **I-V characterisation:** measuring and plotting the forward and reverse characteristics of a p-n junction and a Schottky diode.
3. **C-V profiling:** finding doping concentration and built-in potential from capacitance measurements.
4. **MOSFET parameter extraction:** measuring threshold voltage V_th and channel mobility.

## Assessment

- **CAT 1** (Week 6): crystal structures and carrier statistics (Weeks 1–5).
- **CAT 2** (Week 12): transport, junctions and interfaces (Weeks 7–12).
- **Final examination:** the whole course. The 50-question PGD paper is the best guide to its style.

## How to study each week

1. Read the week's **Symbols and abbreviations** table first. Every week has one near the top, listing every symbol, constant and abbreviation used on that page with its meaning and units, so you can start at any week. The **Glossary** page at the end lists all of them.
2. Read the week's notes once, straight through.
3. Cover the answers and attempt every question on paper.
4. For derivations, check you stated the assumptions; marks are given for them.
5. Tick the week as read (the button at the bottom of each page) to track your progress.

> Numbers to know without looking (E_g is the band-gap energy, n_i the intrinsic carrier concentration, kT/q the thermal voltage, q the electronic charge, μ_n the electron mobility): E_g = 1.12 eV (Si), 0.66 eV (Ge), 1.42 eV (GaAs); n_i(Si) ≈ 1×10¹⁰ cm⁻³; kT/q = 0.0259 V at 300 K; q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C; Si lattice constant 5.43 Å; μ_n(Si) ≈ 1350 cm²/V·s.
