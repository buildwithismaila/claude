The two ways of crossing a barrier, side by side. Thermionic emission needs thermal energy, so it depends strongly on temperature and on barrier height; it gives a rectifying Schottky contact. Tunnelling needs only a thin barrier, which heavy doping provides; it hardly depends on temperature and gives an ohmic contact. Between the two, electrons that are partly heated tunnel through the thinner top part of the barrier: this is called thermionic-field emission.
