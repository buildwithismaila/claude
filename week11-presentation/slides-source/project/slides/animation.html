Animation: how the Schottky barrier forms

Play the animation; it loops every 20 seconds. Step 1: before contact, the semiconductor's Fermi level is higher. Step 2: the materials touch. Step 3: electrons near the interface flow into the metal. Each one leaves behind a positive donor ion that cannot move, so a depletion region grows and an electric field appears, pointing from the ions towards the metal. The semiconductor's Fermi level falls as it loses electrons. Step 4: the flow stops when the Fermi levels are equal. The bands now bend upward near the interface. An electron in the metal faces the barrier q phi Bn; an electron in the semiconductor faces the smaller barrier q V bi. Pause on the last frame to point at the two barriers and the width W.
