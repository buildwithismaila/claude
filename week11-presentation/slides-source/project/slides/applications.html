Applications

Where each contact is used

Schottky contacts

  • Fast, low-loss rectifiers in switching power supplies
  • RF and microwave detectors and mixers
  • Clamp diodes that stop transistors saturating
  • Gates of MESFETs and HEMTs
  • Photodetectors and some solar cells

Ohmic contacts

  • Source, drain and gate of every MOSFET
  • Emitter, base and collector of BJTs
  • Solar-cell contacts: a poor one lowers the fill factor and power
  • LEDs and laser diodes
  • Every connection in an integrated circuit

Schottky contacts are used wherever we want speed or a low forward drop: power-supply rectifiers, RF detectors and mixers, clamp diodes, and the gates of MESFETs and HEMTs. Ohmic contacts are everywhere else: every transistor terminal and every connection on a chip. In a solar cell, a contact that is accidentally rectifying adds series resistance, lowers the fill factor and costs output power. Contacts must also stay stable: aluminium can spike into shallow junctions, and high current densities cause electromigration.
