This example, from our group notes, shows the heavy-doping route in numbers. The formula is the depletion width. With 0.6 V across the region and 10 to the 22 donors per cubic metre, W is 279 nanometres: far too thick to tunnel through, so the contact rectifies. Raise the doping a thousand times, to 10 to the 25, and W falls by the square root of a thousand, 31.6, to 8.8 nanometres. Electrons now tunnel straight through. The barrier height did not change: the thinness alone makes the contact ohmic.
