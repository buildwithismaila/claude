Barrier heights in three cases

First the three formulas: the barrier height for electrons is q times phi m minus chi; the built-in potential is q times phi m minus phi s; and for p-type the hole barrier is the band gap minus q times phi m minus chi. Now substitute. (a) Gold on our silicon: barrier height 1.05 eV, built-in potential 0.84 V; they differ by E_c minus E_F, 0.21 eV. (b) The example from our group notes: 4.70 minus 4.05 gives 0.65 eV. (c) Aluminium on p-type: 1.12 minus 0.23 gives 0.89 eV. So one metal can rectify on p-type and be nearly ohmic on n-type. Real barriers are pinned: gold measures about 0.8 eV.
