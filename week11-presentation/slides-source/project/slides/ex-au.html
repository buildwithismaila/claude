Three quick calculations. (a) Gold, work function 5.10 eV, on our silicon: barrier height 5.10 minus 4.05 is 1.05 eV, and the built-in potential 5.10 minus 4.26 is 0.84 V. They differ by exactly E_c minus E_F, 0.21 eV. (b) The example from our group notes: a metal of 4.70 eV gives 0.65 eV. (c) Aluminium on p-type silicon: the hole barrier is the band gap minus the electron barrier, 1.12 minus 0.23, which is 0.89 eV. So the same metal can rectify on p-type and be nearly ohmic on n-type. Remember real barriers are pinned: gold measures about 0.8 eV.
