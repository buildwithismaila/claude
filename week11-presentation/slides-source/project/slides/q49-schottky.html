The Schottky barrier diode

  • Definition: a metal–semiconductor contact with a barrier to majority carriers, so it rectifies.
  • Before contact (n-type, metal work function larger): the semiconductor’s Fermi level is higher.
  • On contact: electrons flow into the metal, donor ions are left behind, a depletion region forms, the bands bend up, the Fermi levels align.
  • Bias: forward lowers the semiconductor-side barrier, so current rises exponentially; reverse raises it, leaving a small saturation current. The barrier from the metal stays fixed.
  • Transport: thermionic emission of majority carriers: fast, low turn-on voltage, higher leakage.
