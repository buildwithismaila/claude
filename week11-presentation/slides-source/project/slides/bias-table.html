This table sums up the three cases; it is worth copying into your notes for the exam. Note the row "barrier from the metal": fixed in every column. The flow of electrons from the metal into the semiconductor never changes, and under reverse bias it is all that is left, which is why the reverse current saturates. Under forward bias the flow from the semiconductor grows by exp of qV over kT, which is why the forward current rises exponentially.
