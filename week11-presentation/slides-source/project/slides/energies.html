Here are the same ideas as equations. q times phi m is the metal work function: vacuum level minus the metal's Fermi level. q chi is the electron affinity: vacuum level minus the conduction-band edge. The semiconductor work function is the electron affinity plus the gap between E_c and the Fermi level, and that gap depends on doping through E_c minus E_F equals kT ln of N_c over N_D. For n-type silicon with 10 to the 22 donors per cubic metre this gap is 0.205 eV, so the work function is 4.26 eV. Note the band gap is E_c minus E_v, not the other way round.
