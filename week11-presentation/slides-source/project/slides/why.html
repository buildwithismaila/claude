Introduction

Every device needs a metal contact

Schottky contact

Has a barrier, so it rectifies: current flows easily one way only. Used as a fast diode.

Ohmic contact

No barrier that limits current: linear I–V in both directions. Connects every device to the circuit.

ELE 717 · Week 11 · Metal–semiconductor interfaces

3 / 35

When a metal touches a semiconductor, electrons move between them until one Fermi level runs through both. That can leave an energy barrier at the interface. If there is a barrier, the contact rectifies: that is a Schottky contact, named after Walter Schottky, who explained it with Nevill Mott in 1938–39. If there is no barrier that limits the current, the contact is ohmic. A real Schottky diode, shown here, has both: the rectifying contact on top on lightly doped n-type silicon, and an ohmic contact underneath on the heavily doped n-plus substrate.
