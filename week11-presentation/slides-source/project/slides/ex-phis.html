Our first number. First write the formulas: E_c minus E_F equals kT ln of N_c over N_D, and the work function equals the electron affinity plus E_c minus E_F. Now substitute: 0.02585 times ln of 2800, which is 0.02585 times 7.94, gives 0.205 eV. Add the electron affinity, 4.05 eV: the work function is 4.26 eV. We use this silicon for the rest of the talk. Any metal with a larger work function, such as gold at 5.10 eV, forms a Schottky barrier on it.
