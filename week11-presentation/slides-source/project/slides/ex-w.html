Worked example 3

Width and field under the gold contact

Given: built-in potential 0.84 V at zero bias, 1 × 10²² donors m⁻³, silicon permittivity 1.04 × 10⁻¹⁰ F m⁻¹. Find: the depletion width and the peak field.

What this means: the barrier is 0.33 μm thick, far too thick for electrons to tunnel through, so they must go over it. The contact rectifies.

Continue the gold example. With V bi of 0.84 V and 10 to the 22 donors per cubic metre, the depletion width comes out at 3.3 times 10 to the minus 7 metres, 0.33 micrometres. The peak field is 5.1 times 10 to the 6 volts per metre. Keep the 0.33 micrometres in mind: it is about a thousand atoms thick, so electrons cannot tunnel through, and the only way across is over the top. That is why this contact rectifies.
