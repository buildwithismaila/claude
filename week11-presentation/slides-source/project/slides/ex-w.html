Formulas first: the depletion width W is the square root of 2 epsilon s times V bi minus V over q N_D, and the peak field is q N_D W over epsilon s. Substitute for the gold contact at zero bias: W comes out at 3.3 times 10 to the minus 7 metres, 0.33 micrometres, and the peak field at 5.1 times 10 to the 6 volts per metre. 0.33 micrometres is about a thousand atoms thick: electrons cannot tunnel through, so they must go over the top, and the contact rectifies.
