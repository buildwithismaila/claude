Animation: how an ohmic contact forms

The animation loops every 23 seconds and has two scenes. Scene A, steps A1 and A2: a metal whose work function is smaller than the semiconductor's. Before contact the metal's Fermi level is higher, so on contact electrons flow into the semiconductor and pile up at the surface in an accumulation layer. The bands bend down and there is no barrier: electrons move freely both ways. Scene B, steps B1 to B3: the practical method. First a lightly doped contact with a thick barrier: an electron bounces back. Then the doping is raised from 10 to the 22 to 10 to the 25 per cubic metre; the barrier height stays the same but the barrier thins from 279 to 8.8 nanometres. Now electrons tunnel straight through in both directions: the contact is ohmic.
