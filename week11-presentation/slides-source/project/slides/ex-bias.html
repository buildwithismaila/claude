Worked example 4

The depletion width under bias

Given: the gold contact: built-in potential 0.84 V, 1 × 10²² donors m⁻³. Find: the width at 0.30 V forward and at 5 V reverse (zero bias: 3.3 × 10⁻⁷ m).

What this means: forward bias narrows the region to 0.265 μm; 5 V reverse widens it 2.6 times, to 0.871 μm, which also lowers the junction capacitance.

Let us put numbers to the bias picture. In the width formula, V bi minus V becomes 0.84 minus 0.30 under 0.30 V forward bias, and 0.84 plus 5 under 5 V reverse bias. Forward: W is 2.65 times 10 to the minus 7 metres, 0.265 micrometres, narrower than the zero-bias 0.330. Reverse: 8.71 times 10 to the minus 7 metres, 0.871 micrometres, the square root of 5.84 over 0.84 or 2.6 times wider. A wider depletion region means a smaller capacitance, which is what C–V measurement uses next week.
