The formula is the same width formula, with V positive for forward bias and negative for reverse. Under 0.30 V forward, V bi minus V is 0.84 minus 0.30, 0.54 V, giving 2.65 times 10 to the minus 7 metres, 0.265 micrometres. Under 5 V reverse, V is minus 5, so V bi minus V is 0.84 plus 5, 5.84 V, giving 8.71 times 10 to the minus 7 metres, 0.871 micrometres: the square root of 5.84 over 0.84, 2.6 times wider than at zero bias. A wider depletion region means a smaller capacitance, which C–V measurement uses next week.
