Welcome everyone. Today our group presents Week 11: metal–semiconductor interfaces. Every semiconductor device must be connected to the outside world with metal, and what happens where the metal touches the semiconductor decides whether the contact behaves like a diode or like a plain wire. By the end you should be able to draw the band diagrams of both kinds of contact and answer examination question 49.
