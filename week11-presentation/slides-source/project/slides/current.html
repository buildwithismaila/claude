How does current flow? By thermionic emission: majority carriers, electrons for n-type, that have enough thermal energy go over the barrier. The current density is J s times exp of qV over eta kT minus one: the same shape as a p–n diode. The saturation current density J s is A double-star T squared times exp of minus q phi Bn over kT, so it depends exponentially on the barrier height and strongly on temperature. A double-star is the effective Richardson constant. Because the current is carried by majority carriers, no minority charge is stored, so Schottky diodes switch very fast. Real diodes also show some tunnelling, recombination and series resistance, which push the ideality factor above one.
