The ohmic contact, and the comparison

  • Definition: linear, symmetric I–V with negligible resistance.
  • Route 1, accumulation: metal work function smaller (n-type); electrons flow into the semiconductor, the bands bend down, no barrier.
  • Route 2, heavy doping above 10²⁵ m⁻³: the barrier is only a few nm thick and electrons tunnel through it both ways.
  • Good contacts: 10⁻¹⁰ to 10⁻¹² Ω m²; silicides on heavily doped silicon.
  • Finish with the comparison table (slide 22).

279 nm at 10²² m⁻³, only 8.8 nm at 10²⁵ m⁻³

Common mistakes

Saying an ohmic contact has no barrier. Drawing the metal-side barrier changing with bias. Reversing the conditions for p-type.
