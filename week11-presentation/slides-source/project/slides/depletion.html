Inside the depletion region the only charge is the fixed donors, q N_D. Solving Poisson's equation once gives a field that is largest at the interface and falls linearly to zero at W. Solving again gives the parabolic band bending. The width is the square root of 2 epsilon s times V bi minus V over q N_D. Two things to remember: reverse bias, negative V, makes W wider; and W goes as one over the square root of N_D, so heavy doping makes the region very thin. That second fact is how practical ohmic contacts work. The depletion region also behaves like a capacitor, which is the basis of C–V measurement next week.
